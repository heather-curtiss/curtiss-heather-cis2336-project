Frequently Asked Questions:

What is ArtConnect's purpose?

ArtConnect's purpose is to give artists and art enthusiasts of all types a platform to post art, locate events, and connect with other like-minded people!

How do I submit artwork?

You can use the submission form on the Submit Art page.

Does it cost money to use ArtConnect?

No. ArtConnect is free for all users.

How can I find events?

Events are listed on the Events page.

How do I contact ArtConnect?

You can reach us using the information listed on the Developer Contact section on the homepage.
